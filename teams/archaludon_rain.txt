# Archaludon rain, two modes (Pokémon Champions VGC, Regulation M-C)
# Rain mode: Archaludon + Pelipper + Mega Swampert + Basculegion-F (fast offense, Raichu Y / Floette / Sylveon / mirrors).
# Second mode: Archaludon + Pelipper + Gliscor + Mega Gengar (Rillaboom, Trick Room, sun / sand).
# Gliscor took the slot because it stacks none of the mode's weaknesses (Ground: Archaludon + Gengar,
# Fighting: Archaludon, Electric / Rock: Pelipper, Psychic / Ghost / Dark: Gengar) and is immune to Ground and
# Electric. Its own weaknesses are Water (boosted by our rain) and Ice: keep it away from rain and snow teams.
# SPs = Stat Points (max 32 per stat, 66 total). One Mega per game: Swampert in rain mode, Gengar in the second.

# Mega Garchomp-Z, Mega Delphox). Mega Gengar 182 beats Sneasler (172) and Mega Staraptor (178).
