# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- set notes
# Staraptor: Jolly 178 outspeeds Sneasler (172), Volcarona, Mega Metagross, Ninetales-Alola; bulk SPs saved nothing.
#   After one Close Combat (+1 Def / SpD) only 5 of the top 70 OHKO it (16 at +0). Tailwind kept over Helping Hand /
#   Quick Attack (tied in the model); Protect is the slot that matters.
# Tyranitar: one spread for both forms. Fast Jolly spreads let 13 of the top 70 OHKO the Mega (7 here). Ice Punch
#   OHKOs Mega Salamence and hits Mega Garchomp Z 76% / Mega Dragonite 65%, which nothing else covers; Low Kick
#   overlaps Close Combat. Stone Edge is the alternative if Pelipper is a problem (80% accurate).
# Gholdengo: 18 HP stops 7 OHKOs (Incineroar, Mega Charizard Y, Mega Froslass, Dragapult, Hydreigon, Mega Mawile,
#   Talonflame); 120 Speed doubles to 240 in Tailwind, ahead of Basculegion / Dragapult / Indeedee. Power Gem over
#   Nasty Plot scored +0.08 in the lead model (Pelipper, Incineroar) but the model undervalues setup.
# Rillaboom: High Horsepower OHKOs Sneasler (121%) and Mega Gengar, 81% into Gholdengo. Don't Fake Out Archaludon
#   (Stamina): Fake Out Pelipper and keep Archaludon at +0 Def for Close Combat. 107 Speed beats the 105 mirror.
# Milotic: Modest (9 OHKOs at +0, 37 after Competitive) costs one OHKO taken vs Calm. Sitrus: survives two hits of
#   up to 59% (50% with Leftovers); it is on the field ~2.8 turns, too short for Leftovers to pay. Recover = Protect.
# Volcarona: an attacker, not a redirector (Overheat 1.3 / Rage Powder 0.24 uses per game in the sim). Bulky
#   18 / 16 / 32 beat full SpA by +0.9 in the sim; Focus Sash beat Charcoal (-1.7), Expert Belt (-1.9), Charti (-0.7).
#   Its value: Mega Charizard Y + Mega Golisopod (+1.7 vs a redirector in its slot), Incineroar + Mega Metagross.
