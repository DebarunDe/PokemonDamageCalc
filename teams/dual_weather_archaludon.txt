# Dual weather: Archaludon rain + Mega Charizard Y sun (Pokemon Champions VGC, Regulation M-C). Full team report.
#
# Evidence used throughout:
#   real    tournament teams (meta.sqlite): wins per 100 games above the players' skill (+ = better than expected)
#   sim     battle sim (analysis/doubles_sim.py), full games vs 2,548 real tournament teams, paired comparisons
#   model   lead model (analysis/leads.py), turns 1-2 game-theoretic, bring-4 with switching, 18 commonest leads
# The sim's AI underplays screens and Sleep Powder and the lead model has no screens, so Grimmsnarl and Venusaur's
# value is mostly in the real results.
#
# ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ provenance
# Archaludon: 3rd of 50 common species by strength above expectation, +1.8 ± 0.3 (tournaments +1.7, ladder +2.2),
#   the most reliable strong non-Mega in the format. Mega Charizard Y: 5th, +1.7 ± 0.3.
# This six is the commonest dual-weather build: 124 tournament teams with exactly these six at +4.9 (about ± 1.6);
#   all Archaludon + Pelipper + Mega Charizard Y teams: 883 at +1.5 ± 0.6. One Mega only: the Mega Garchomp Z
#   version (388 teams) does +1.6; a second stone that rarely Mega Evolves costs more than it adds.
# Our change from the classic sets: Archaludon Bold 32 HP / 32 SpD instead of Modest (real Bold +2.8 / Calm +1.5 /
#   Modest +0.8 on dual-weather teams; across all Archaludon teams +2.1 / +1.0 / -0.8; sim +6.8 over the classic).
#   Pelipper Bold with Wide Guard (real +2.4 Bold vs -0.6 Modest; Wide Guard +1.7 vs +0.6 without).
# Result: sim 61.7% with the sim's own team preview (classic six 54.7%); 66-67% when leading one of the three
#   core leads below. Lead model +1.72 average, 1 losing lead of 18.
#
# ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ theory
# Choose the weather war you can win at team preview. Two weather engines, so against nearly every team one of
# them is uncontested:
#   SUN   Mega Charizard Y + Venusaur. Drought starts when Charizard Mega Evolves, after leads are in, so it
#         replaces a lead Pelipper / Politoed / Tyranitar's weather on turn 1. Sun doubles Venusaur's Speed
#         (Chlorophyll): Sleep Powder and Leaf Storm before most of the format. Heat Wave hits both foes.
#   RAIN  Pelipper + Archaludon. Rain removes Electro Shot's charge turn (+1 SpA and the hit on the same turn);
#         Pelipper's Tailwind fixes Archaludon's 105 Speed; Hurricane never misses in rain.
#   Archaludon is the anchor in any weather: Stamina + Leftovers + 32 HP / 32 SpD, Snarl for special attackers.
#   Outside rain it charges behind screens. In the sim it scores 30% of our KOs (Electro Shot is the top KO
#   move, 20%) and is still standing at the end of 63% of wins.
#   Grimmsnarl buys the turns both modes need: Prankster Reflect / Light Screen (8 turns with Light Clay) cover
#   Archaludon's charge turn, Venusaur's Sleep Powder turn and a setter coming back in. Spirit Break for Dragons,
#   Parting Shot to pivot. Real dual-weather teams with it: +1.8; without: -0.5.
#   Choice Scarf Garchomp covers what neither weather does: fast in any weather, Earthquake into Raichu,
#   Archaludon mirrors, Incineroar, Gholdengo, Kingambit, Mega Metagross; Rock Slide into Charizard and
#   Volcarona. Charizard and Pelipper are immune to its Earthquake; Archaludon is not.
# Bonus: their weather works for us too. Into rain teams, their own rain fires our Electro Shot immediately;
#   into sun teams, their sun gives Venusaur Chlorophyll.
# SPs = Stat Points (max 32 per stat, 66 total). One Mega: Charizard Y.

# ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ set notes
# (real = dual-weather tournament teams, wins per 100 above skill; sim = vs the classic-plus-Bold baseline)
# Archaludon  Bold > Calm > Modest (real +2.8 / +1.5 / +0.8). Snarl over Flash Cannon: real +3.3 with Snarl,
#             +0.7 with Flash Cannon (sim +0.6 for Flash Cannon is noise). Avoid Aura Sphere (-0.9).
# Pelipper    Bold > Modest / Timid (+2.4 / -0.6 / -2.1); Calm +3.0 on only 77 teams. Wide Guard +1.7 vs +0.6
#             without; it blocks Rock Slide (4x on Charizard), Earthquake (Archaludon), Heat Wave, Hyper Voice.
#             Tailwind is essential: teams without it -2.3. Sitrus +2.1 vs Focus Sash -1.7.
# Charizard   Modest (+2.4, 343 teams) over Timid (+0.9, 533); the sim leans Timid (+0.9 ± 0.5). Timid's 167
#             Speed outspeeds Modest Charizard mirrors (152): switch if mirrors are common. Never Solar Beam
#             (-3.7): Hurricane is the coverage that works in both weathers.
# Venusaur    Modest, Focus Sash, Leaf Storm, Sleep Powder: all better in the real data. Earth Power over Leaf
#             Storm worse in both (real -0.6 vs +2.0, sim -1.2). Life Orb +2.9 on only 41 teams.
# Grimmsnarl  Impish (+3.5) over Sassy (+1.7) and Careful (+1.2); the sim agrees. Taunt / Thunder Wave / Fake
#             Out over Parting Shot: no gain.
# Garchomp    Adamant Choice Scarf: best in both (Jolly -1.5 in the sim; Life Orb / Sash + Protect -2 to -3.7).
#             Every replacement for Garchomp tested in the sim lost 2-12 points.
# Not adopted: the sim's Grimmsnarl -> Sneasler (+8.6 in the sim) is contradicted by real teams (Sneasler -3.3,
#   28 teams; without Grimmsnarl -0.5). Hisuian Arcanine over Venusaur (+5 sim, model +1.87 with no losing
#   lead) has never been tried by a real team, loses the Grass answer to rain and is 4x weak to our own
#   Earthquake. Worth testing in practice, not proven.
#

# ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ weaknesses
# No Fake Out and no Trick Room answer beyond screens and our slow, bulky half.
# Charizard is 4x weak to Rock and Pelipper 4x to Electric: Wide Guard and Reflect soften Rock Slide; Garchomp
#   handles the Electric types.
# Mega Charizard Y + Mega Garchomp Z is the one losing lead in the model (-0.59): Garchomp Z outspeeds and hits
#   Archaludon and Charizard hard. Grimmsnarl's Spirit Break and screens are the answer.
# Rain is easy to lose: in the sim it is up only 22% of turns. Bring Pelipper back in to reset rain, and time
#   Tailwind with Electro Shot.
# Garchomp's Earthquake hits our own Archaludon (2x) and Venusaur; pair Garchomp with Charizard or Pelipper.
#
# ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ matchup guide
# Bring-4 and lead per archetype: battle sim with all four and the lead forced, about 60 combinations tested per
# archetype (± 1-2 points; sand ± 3; the best of 60 is a little flattered by noise). Damage = average %, in the
# weather noted. Default four if unsure: Charizard, Garchomp, Archaludon, Pelipper (73.2% over all teams).
#
# TAILWIND OFFENCE (25%: Rillaboom 84%, Raichu Y 50%, Gholdengo 44%, Incineroar 43%, Volcarona 41%, Sneasler)
#   Bring Charizard, Venusaur, Archaludon, Garchomp. Lead Charizard + Venusaur. 71.8%
#   T1 Mega Evolve: sun. Heat Wave hits Rillaboom 225%, Gholdengo 235%; Hurricane Sneasler 201%. Venusaur
#   (Chlorophyll) Sleep Powders Raichu Y or Sneasler before they move. Protect Venusaur when Volcarona or
#   Incineroar can fire (239% / 187%). Back: Scarf Garchomp revenge-kills Raichu Y (Stomping Tantrum 182%,
#   immune to Zap Cannon, which does 150% to Charizard), Sneasler (Earthquake 148%), Volcarona (Rock Slide 135%).
#   Archaludon tanks and charges Electro Shot to close.
#
# SUN (23%: Charizard Y 81%, Garchomp 43%, Venusaur 39%, Kingambit, Archaludon, Farigiraf)
#   Bring Venusaur, Garchomp, Archaludon, Charizard. Lead Venusaur + Garchomp. 70.8%
#   Their sun gives our Venusaur Chlorophyll: Sleep Powder their biggest threat. Garchomp Rock Slides their
#   Charizard (124%). Their Weather Ball does 229% to Venusaur: Focus Sash, or Protect on the Mega turn.
#   Back: Archaludon takes Charizard's Fire for 31% and fires back (Electro Shot 150%, after its charge turn);
#   our Charizard takes the sun back if it is lost and Weather Ball handles Kingambit (194%). Don't bring
#   Pelipper + Charizard together into sun.
#

#   Reflect / Light Screen first (Prankster) against Golisopod and Sneasler. Back: Charizard Mega Evolves and its
#   sun shuts their rain off: Weather Ball does 331% to Golisopod and Swift Swim stops. Garchomp: Earthquake into
#   Sneasler (148%) and Raichu X; watch Basculegion-F's Ice Beam (128% to Garchomp) and Raichu X's Volt Tackle
