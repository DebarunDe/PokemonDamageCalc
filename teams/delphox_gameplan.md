# Game plan: Mega Delphox / Mega Dragonite (Sneasler version)

```
Delphox @ Delphoxite / Blaze / 2 HP / 32 SpA / 32 Spe / Timid: Heat Wave, Psychic, Nasty Plot, Protect
Dragonite @ Dragoninite / Multiscale / 2 HP / 32 SpA / 32 Spe / Modest: Dragon Pulse, Heat Wave, Tailwind, Protect
Sinistcha @ Colbur Berry / Hospitality / 32 HP / 32 Def / 2 SpA / Bold: Matcha Gotcha, Rage Powder, Trick Room, Protect
Incineroar @ Sitrus Berry / Intimidate / 32 HP / 14 Def / 20 SpD / Careful: Fake Out, Flare Blitz, Throat Chop, Parting Shot
Kingambit @ Chople Berry / Defiant / 32 HP / 32 Atk / 2 SpD / Adamant: Kowtow Cleave, Sucker Punch, Iron Head, Protect
Sneasler @ Focus Sash / Poison Touch / 2 HP / 32 Atk / 32 Spe / Jolly: Fake Out, Close Combat, Poison Jab, Quick Guard
```

Speed: Delphox-Mega 204, Sneasler 189, Dragonite-Mega 152 (304 in Tailwind), Sinistcha 90, Incineroar 80, Kingambit 70.

## Fake Out does not work on

- Ghost types: Basculegion, Gholdengo, Sinistcha, Mega Gengar.
- Anything next to Farigiraf (Armor Tail blocks priority for both foes).
- Grounded targets in Psychic Terrain (Indeedee-F).
- Inner Focus Pokémon, and anything holding Covert Cloak.

Sucker Punch is blocked the same way by Armor Tail and Psychic Terrain.

## Rules of thumb

- Choose the Mega at team preview. Delphox is the default; use Dragonite against rain, sun, or two or more dragons.
- Never Mega Evolve Delphox into a priority move you haven't covered (Quick Guard, Fake Out or Protect first).
- Keep Kingambit in the back so Defiant triggers off their Intimidate.
- Don't count on Heat Wave in rain (halved) or against Pelipper's Wide Guard.
